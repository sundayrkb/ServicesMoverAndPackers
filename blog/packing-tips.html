Before you start, gather these materials. Quality supplies prevent damage and save time.

Pro Tip: Get 20% more boxes than you think. Running out mid-pack wastes hours.

Smart Box Labeling System

Our 3-part label method ensures boxes land in the right room and unpack in priority order.

🔴 KITCHEN — Room (color-coded)
HIGH — Priority (H=first, M=week 1, L=later)
Plates, bowls, glasses — Contents summary

Color Code by Room:

  • Red = Kitchen
  • Blue = Bathroom
  • Green = Bedroom
  • Yellow = Living Room
  • Purple = Office/Study
  • Orange = Storage/Garage

Tape label on top and two sides so visible when stacked.

Fragile Item Protection

Dishes & Glassware

  1. Line box bottom with crumpled paper
  2. Wrap each piece individually in packing paper
  3. Stack plates vertically (on edge), not flat
  4. Fill all gaps with paper — zero movement
  5. Mark "FRAGILE — THIS SIDE UP" on all sides

Mirrors, Art & TV Screens

Electronics

Original boxes are best. If not, double-box with 2" cushion on all sides. Remove batteries.

Clothing Packing Hacks

Wardrobe Boxes

Hang clothes directly — no folding, no wrinkles. One box holds ~2 ft of hanging space.

Dresser Drawers

Leave clothes in drawers. Wrap entire dresser with stretch wrap. Movers carry as-is.

Vacuum Bags

Off-season coats, blankets, pillows compress 75%. Label "VACUUM — DO NOT STACK."

Suitcases

Use luggage for heavy items: books, shoes, tools. Wheels make transport effortless.

Electronics & Cables

  1. Photograph connections before unplugging (back of TV, router, desktop)
  2. Bag & label cables per device: "TV — HDMI, Power, Ethernet"
  3. Remove ink cartridges from printers; seal in bags
  4. Backup drives — carry separately
  5. Original packaging = best protection. Keep for high-value items.

Unpacking Strategy: Room by Room

Priority Order:

  1. Bedroom: Bed frame, mattress, bedding → sleep ready
  2. Bathroom: Toiletries, towels, shower curtain
  3. Kitchen: Coffee maker, 2 plates, 2 cups, utensils
  4. Living Room: Sofa, essentials only
  5. Everything else: One room at a time
Pro Tip: Unpack HIGH priority boxes first. Leave LOW boxes sealed until needed — reduces clutter.
