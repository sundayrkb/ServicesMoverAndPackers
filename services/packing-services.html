Frequently Asked Questions

Can I hire just packing without moving?

Absolutely. Our packing services are available standalone. Many customers use us for packing, then use their own transport or another mover. We provide a detailed inventory for handover.

How long does packing take?

Typical timeline: 1 BHK — 2-3 hours, 2 BHK — 3-4 hours, 3 BHK — 4-6 hours, 4+ BHK/Villa — 6-10 hours. Office: 50 seats — 4-6 hours. We provide exact estimate after survey.

Do you pack on the same day as moving?

Yes, we can pack and move same day for local moves (crew arrives early morning). For better organization, we recommend packing day before move day. Multi-day options available.

What about items I want to pack myself?

No problem. We'll pack everything except designated "owner-packed" zones. Just label the room/boxes. We'll note exceptions on inventory. Valuables, documents, medications should stay with you.

Can you unpack at my new home?

Yes, our unpacking service includes: room-wise unpacking per your direction, furniture assembly, basic appliance setup, wardrobe organization, kitchen cabinet arrangement, picture/mirror hanging (additional), and complete debris removal.

Is there insurance for packed items?

Yes, transit insurance covers packed items up to declared value (typically 1.5-3% premium). Professional packing actually reduces insurance premiums because damage risk is lower. We handle claims documentation.
