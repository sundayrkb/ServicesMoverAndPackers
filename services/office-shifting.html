Frequently Asked Questions

How do you ensure zero data loss during server moves?

We follow a strict protocol: 1) Verified backup completion before shutdown, 2) Anti-static, shock-mounted transport for drives, 3) Climate-controlled vehicles, 4) Rack-level reassembly with cable mapping, 5) Post-move RAID integrity and application testing before sign-off.

Can you move our office over a single weekend?

Yes, for offices up to 200 seats we typically complete Friday 6 PM to Monday 8 AM. Larger offices may need phased approach or extended weekend. We provide detailed timeline during survey.

Do you handle building management approvals?

Absolutely. We coordinate with both building managements for: loading bay reservations, elevator scheduling, parking permits, security clearances, insurance certificates, and noise/dust compliance.

How do you handle employee personal items?

We provide personal packing kits (boxes, labels, bubble wrap) one week before. Employees pack personal items; we move labeled boxes to assigned new workstations. Valuables stay with employees.

What if we need temporary storage during renovation?

We offer climate-controlled warehousing with 24/7 security. Inventory management with photo documentation. Flexible weekly/monthly rates. Staged delivery as floors become ready.

Do you provide post-move cleaning?

Yes, we partner with professional cleaning services for both old and new premises. Deep cleaning, carpet sanitization, glass cleaning, waste disposal — coordinated as part of the move package.
