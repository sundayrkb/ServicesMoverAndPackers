Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book home relocation?

We recommend booking 7-10 days in advance for preferred dates. Peak months (April-June, October-December) book faster — 2-3 weeks ahead is ideal. We accommodate urgent moves within 48 hours when possible.

Do you provide packing materials or should I buy them?

We provide all premium packing materials as part of the service: corrugated boxes (various sizes), bubble wrap, packing paper, furniture blankets, stretch film, tape, markers, and specialized cartons (wardrobe, dish packs, TV boxes). No need to purchase anything.

What items cannot be moved?

We cannot transport: flammables (paint, aerosols, fuel), perishables, live plants, pets, valuables (jewelry, cash, important documents), hazardous chemicals, and weapons. We'll provide a complete list during the survey.

How is transit insurance calculated?

Insurance premium is typically 1.5-3% of declared value. You declare the replacement value of goods. Claims require photos and are settled within 72 hours. We recommend insuring high-value items separately.

Can you move on weekends or holidays?

Yes, we operate 7 days a week including most holidays. Weekend/holiday moves may have a 10-15% surcharge. Sunday moves are popular — book early.

What if my new home isn't ready on delivery day?

We offer free storage for up to 7 days at our secure warehouse. Beyond that, nominal daily charges apply. We'll coordinate re-delivery once your home is ready.
